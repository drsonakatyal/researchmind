The Three Non-Negotiable Rules

1. DISCLOSEDocument the name, version, and purpose of every AI tool used in the methodology.
2. LOGMaintain a research diary of all significant AI prompts and outputs.
3. OWNYou must be able to defend every argument in a viva without AI.

AI Use Classification

🟢 GREEN (Ethical): Grammar checking, literature mapping, concept explanation. (Disclosure Required)
🟡 AMBER (Caution): AI-generated first drafts or summaries. (Must be substantially rewritten and verified)
🔴 RED (Violation): Submitting AI text as own work, fabrications, or sharing private data. (Academic Misconduct)
