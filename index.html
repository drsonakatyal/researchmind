The Three Non-Negotiable Rules

Regardless of the AI tool used, these three rules apply universally to all academic research:

1. DISCLOSEDocument the name, version, and purpose of every AI tool used.
2. LOGMaintain an audit trail of prompts and outputs as evidence of responsible use.
3. OWNEvery claim must be traceable to your own judgment. You must be able to defend it.

AI Use Classification

Use this framework to self-assess the ethical risk of your AI integration:

🟢 GREEN (Ethical): Grammar checking, literature mapping, and statistical analysis. (Disclosure Required)
🟡 AMBER (Caution): AI-generated first drafts or summaries. (Substantial rewriting & disclosure required)
🔴 RED (Violation): Submitting AI text as your own or using fabricated citations. (Academic Misconduct)

Precision Prompting Formula

To ensure reliability and reduce hallucinations, use this 4-part structure for every prompt:

ROLE → TASK → CONTEXT → CONSTRAINT

Example: "Act as a peer reviewer (Role), identify limitations (Task), for a PhD in Economics (Context), using only peer-reviewed sources (Constraint)."
